MEET OUR CEO

Josephine Okafor, RN

Chief Executive Officer

Josephine Okafor is the Chief Executive Officer of Amazing Support LLC and a Registered Nurse who brings healthcare knowledge, thoughtful leadership, and a strong sense of responsibility to the company. Her nursing background has taught her how important safety, dignity, clear communication, observation, and respect are when supporting another person. She uses that experience to help shape the standards of Amazing Support LLC and to keep the company focused on dependable, person-centered service.

As CEO, Josephine supports the company by guiding staff, encouraging teamwork, helping solve problems, and making sure caregivers understand the importance of following each client’s service plan. She believes good leadership means being available, listening carefully, communicating clearly, and creating an environment where both clients and employees feel respected. She wants caregivers to feel prepared and supported so they can provide steady, professional assistance in every home they enter.

Her experience as an RN also helps her recognize why even small changes in a client’s condition or behavior can matter. Amazing Support LLC provides non-medical home support, but Josephine understands the importance of noticing concerns, reporting them promptly, documenting services correctly, and making sure the right person is informed when something changes. She encourages the team to pay attention, respect client choices, maintain professional boundaries, and place safety at the center of everyday support.

Josephine is committed to building a company that families can trust and employees can be proud to represent. She continues to support the growth of Amazing Support LLC while keeping its purpose clear: helping people remain comfortable, connected, and as independent as possible in their own homes. Her leadership combines the compassion of nursing with the organization and accountability needed to operate a dependable home support company.
