SERVICE DETAILS

Personal Care

Learn what this service can include, how it supports everyday life, and how it may be included in an individualized service plan.

What Personal Care Means at Amazing Support LLC

Personal care support is designed for individuals who may need extra help with daily routines while still wanting to stay involved in their own choices and routines. Our team can assist with bathing, grooming, dressing, toileting, hygiene, and other everyday activities in a respectful and private way.

We focus on helping each person maintain dignity, comfort, and as much independence as possible. Support is based on the client’s service plan and personal preferences, and caregivers are expected to communicate respectfully, protect privacy, and follow established routines.

Personal care can also include help preparing for the day, setting out clothing, assisting with footwear, providing routine reminders, and making sure frequently used items are within reach. The goal is to make everyday routines safer and easier without taking over tasks a client can still do independently.

Common Ways This Service May Help

  • Bathing and hygiene support
  • Dressing and undressing assistance
  • Toileting and incontinence support
  • Grooming and oral hygiene
  • Routine positioning and personal comfort

How We Approach This Service

Services are provided according to the client’s individualized needs, preferences, and documented service plan. Caregivers are expected to support independence, communicate respectfully, report concerns, and stay within the non-medical scope of their role.

If a client’s needs change, the company may review the service plan and determine whether additional support, reassessment, or coordination is needed.
