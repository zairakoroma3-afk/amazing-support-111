SERVICE DETAILS

Personal Care

Personal care support is designed for individuals who need extra help with daily routines while still wanting to remain involved in their own choices and care. Our caregivers focus on dignity, privacy, comfort, and independence during every visit.

REQUEST THIS SERVICE →

What This Service Can Include

  • Bathing, shower, or routine hygiene assistance within the service plan
  • Grooming, oral hygiene, hair care, and getting ready for the day
  • Dressing and undressing support, including footwear and clothing setup
  • Toileting support and routine incontinence hygiene assistance
  • Non-clinical eating assistance when included in the service plan
  • Routine positioning, transfers, and comfort support within caregiver scope

How Amazing Support LLC Approaches Personal Care

Every client has different preferences about how personal routines should be completed. We take time to understand those preferences and encourage caregivers to support the client rather than take over tasks the client can still perform independently.

Caregivers are expected to protect privacy, explain what they are doing, communicate respectfully, and report meaningful changes in condition or ability to the appropriate supervisor.

The exact activities provided during a visit are based on the individualized service plan, caregiver role, client preference, and the company’s non-medical scope of service.

Individualized Service Planning

The exact tasks provided during visits depend on the client’s needs, preferences, household routine, caregiver scope, and written service plan. Not every client needs every task listed on this page, and support can be adjusted when needs change.

Communication and Reporting

Caregivers are expected to communicate respectfully, document services as required, and report meaningful concerns or changes to the appropriate supervisor. If a client begins needing support outside the company’s non-medical scope, leadership may discuss appropriate next steps with the client or family.

Personal Care at a Glance

Respectful assistance with bathing, grooming, dressing, toileting, hygiene, and other daily personal routines.
