CLIENT & FAMILY RESOURCES

Information That Helps Families Understand Home Support.

Use these resources to prepare for service, understand the service plan, communicate changes, and know what to expect from non-medical support.

01

Preparing for Home Support

Think about the daily routines that are most difficult, preferred visit times, personal preferences, mobility concerns, household routines, and who should be contacted when questions arise.

02

Understanding the Service Plan

The service plan describes what support is expected, how frequently services occur, and the tasks caregivers should focus on during visits.

03

Reporting Changes

Clients and families should communicate meaningful changes in condition, routine, mobility, safety, schedule, or service needs so the company can review the situation.

04

Caregiver Communication

Caregivers should communicate respectfully, follow the service plan, document services, and report concerns according to company procedures.

05

Questions and Concerns

If something is unclear, contact Amazing Support LLC. Clear questions and early communication can help prevent misunderstandings and keep services aligned with client needs.

06

When Needs Change

A change in a client’s condition or ability may require follow-up, reassessment, or discussion about whether current services remain appropriate.
