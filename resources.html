Helpful Information for Clients and Families

This section is designed to give clients and families a clearer understanding of home support services, communication expectations, service planning, and how to prepare for care.

Preparing for Home Support

Think about the routines, tasks, preferences, schedules, and concerns that are most important to the person receiving support.

Communicating Changes

Families should report meaningful changes in condition, schedule, safety, or service needs so the company can review the situation appropriately.

Understanding the Service Plan

The service plan explains what support is expected, how often it is provided, and what caregivers should focus on during visits.
