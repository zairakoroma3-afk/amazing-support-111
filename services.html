OUR SERVICES

Detailed Support Options for Different Everyday Needs.

Each service page explains what the service may include, how it can help, and how it fits within an individualized non-medical home support plan.

Support Should Be Clear Before It Begins.

Families should not have to guess what a service means. We explain our service categories in detail so clients can understand the type of help that may be available and discuss which tasks belong in the individualized service plan.

Personal Care

Personal care support is designed for individuals who need extra help with daily routines while still wanting to remain involved in their own choices and care. Our caregivers focus on dignity, privacy, comfort, and independence during every visit.

Every client has different preferences about how personal routines should be completed. We take time to understand those preferences and encourage caregivers to support the client rather than take over tasks the client can still perform independently.

Companionship

Companionship is more than simply being present. It is about building a respectful connection that helps a client feel seen, heard, engaged, and less isolated.

Caregivers are encouraged to learn what each client enjoys and to create appropriate activities around those interests. Some clients prefer conversation; others enjoy music, reading, television, games, or simply having someone nearby.

READ FULL SERVICE DETAILS →

Household Support

Household support helps clients keep their everyday living space cleaner, safer, and easier to manage when routine chores become difficult.

This service is focused on routine household tasks connected to the client’s daily living needs. It is not intended to replace heavy-duty commercial cleaning, major home maintenance, or specialty services.

READ FULL SERVICE DETAILS →

Mobility Assistance

Mobility assistance helps clients complete everyday movement more safely while encouraging them to participate as much as they are able.

Caregivers follow the client’s service plan and use safe techniques within the limits of their role and training.

READ FULL SERVICE DETAILS →

Medication Reminders

Medication reminder support is limited to non-clinical reminders and routine assistance for medications that the client manages and takes independently.

Caregivers do not independently prescribe medications, change dosages, make clinical medication decisions, or perform tasks outside the authorization of their role.

READ FULL SERVICE DETAILS →

Nutrition Support

Nutrition support helps make regular meals easier to maintain by providing practical help before, during, and after mealtimes.

Caregivers should respect cultural preferences, food choices, household routines, and documented restrictions or instructions.

READ FULL SERVICE DETAILS →

Respite Care

Respite care supports both the client and the family caregiver by providing dependable temporary assistance in the home.

Family caregiving can be meaningful and demanding. Respite support provides time for family caregivers to rest, manage responsibilities, or step away knowing that agreed-upon non-medical support is in place.

READ FULL SERVICE DETAILS →

Oversight & Safety

Oversight and safety support adds another layer of everyday awareness for clients who may benefit from someone noticing environmental risks or changes in routine.

This service does not replace clinical monitoring or emergency medical care. It is focused on everyday observation and communication within the non-medical caregiver role.
