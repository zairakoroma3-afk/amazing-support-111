REFERRALS

Refer Someone Who May Benefit From Home Support.

Families, professionals, community organizations, and other referral sources may contact Amazing Support LLC to discuss non-medical support needs.

Referral Information

A referral should include enough information for our team to understand who needs support, what type of assistance is being requested, how to contact the appropriate person, and whether there are scheduling or safety concerns that may affect service planning.

After receiving referral information, the company can discuss requested services and determine whether the need appears to fit within our non-medical scope and current availability.

Helpful Details to Include

  • Client name and best contact information
  • Primary contact or family representative
  • Requested service type
  • Preferred schedule or start timeframe
  • Important mobility or household considerations
  • Any known communication preferences
