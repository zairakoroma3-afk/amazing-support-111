FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to Common Questions About Our Services.

What type of services does Amazing Support LLC provide?

We provide non-medical home support such as personal care, companionship, household support, mobility assistance, meal support, medication reminders for self-managed medications, respite care, and everyday safety observation.

Is Amazing Support LLC a nursing service?

Our listed services are non-medical home support services. Josephine Okafor is a Registered Nurse and serves as CEO, but caregiver services are provided according to the company’s non-medical scope and the client’s service plan.

How do I request care?

Use the Request Care page, call 703-870-9966, or email Amazingsupport26@gmail.com. The team can discuss the type of help being requested and next steps.

Can services be customized?

Yes. The exact tasks included depend on the client’s needs, preferences, household routines, and individualized service plan.

What happens if a client’s needs change?

Meaningful changes should be reported so leadership can review the situation and determine whether the service plan, schedule, or level of support should be reconsidered.

How can I apply for a job?

Use the Apply page to submit your contact information and role of interest.
