Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Questions

What type of care does Amazing Support LLC provide?

We provide non-medical home support services such as personal care, companionship, light household support, meal assistance, mobility help, medication reminders, respite support, and safety observation.

Do you provide nursing care?

Amazing Support LLC focuses on non-medical support. The CEO is a Registered Nurse, but services are provided according to the company’s non-medical scope and applicable requirements.

How do I request care?

Use the Request Care form or call 703-870-9966.

Can I apply online?

Yes. Use the Apply page to submit your information.
