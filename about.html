ABOUT AMAZING SUPPORT LLC

Home Support Built Around the Person, Not Just the Task.

We provide non-medical support for individuals who want help with everyday routines while continuing to live with comfort, dignity, and independence at home.

Who We Are

Amazing Support LLC provides practical non-medical home support services for people who need assistance with personal routines, household tasks, companionship, mobility, meals, respite, reminders, or everyday safety awareness.

We understand that two clients can request the same service but need very different types of support. One client may need hands-on help with dressing while another may only need clothing set out and a little extra time. One person may enjoy long conversations during companionship visits while another may prefer quiet activities, reading, or walks.

That is why our approach is centered on the individual. We focus on learning what matters to the client, understanding household routines, clarifying family communication preferences, and providing support according to an individualized service plan.

What Good Home Support Means to Us

Good home support should be respectful, dependable, and easy to understand. Caregivers should know what tasks they are expected to complete, clients should understand what services include, and families should know who to contact when they have questions or when something changes.

We also believe clients should remain involved in their own routines whenever possible. Support should help people do more safely and comfortably, not automatically take over activities they are still able and willing to do.

Communication Matters

Changes in condition, mobility, mood, appetite, routines, safety, or service needs can affect the type of support a client requires. Caregivers are expected to observe, document, and report meaningful concerns according to company procedures so leadership can determine whether follow-up is needed.

What Clients Can Expect

  • Respect for personal preferences
  • Clear service expectations
  • Support based on an individualized plan
  • Professional caregiver conduct
  • Attention to everyday safety
  • Communication when concerns arise
  • Leadership support when needs change
